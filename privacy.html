First Chair: Ski Builder — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

First Chair is a game made by Zigmas Reisas. It does not ask for an account, your name, your email or your location, and it shows no adverts.

What is stored on your device

Your scores, badges, settings and any game in progress are saved on your device only.

Purchases

If you buy or restore the Season Pass, the purchase is handled by Apple. We use a service called RevenueCat to confirm that the purchase is valid. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and the purchase record from Apple. It does not receive your name, email or payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Tracking

The game does not track you across other apps or websites.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
