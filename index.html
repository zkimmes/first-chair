Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email zigmasreisas@gmail.com and you will get a reply.

Common questions

I bought the Season Pass and it is locked again. Open the Season Pass screen and tap "Restore purchases". Make sure you are signed in with the Apple ID you bought it with.

How do I get a refund? Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Is my progress saved? Yes, on your device. Deleting the app deletes scores and any game in progress; the Season Pass can always be restored.
